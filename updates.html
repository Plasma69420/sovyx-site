What's new at Sovyx

Every release, written down as we ship it.

v1.2.0
November 23, 2026

HWID locks that actually lock.

Your software can now sign buyers in, so keys lock to a PC and live sessions fill in for real.

  • NewSign-in API: /api/v1/login, heartbeat and logout. The first login starts the key's countdown, locks it to the PC and opens a session.
  • NewBuyers can reset their own HWID from your customer page or the API, with an on/off switch and a cooldown you choose.
  • NewVariables come back in every login, so you can change settings without a new build.
  • NewReseller and staff panels for each product, with credits, an end date, and permissions for HWID resets, bans, sessions and an activity log.
  • NewA how-to with your App ID filled in on the API keys page.
  • ImprovedThe home page and these pages were redesigned.
v1.1.0
November 22, 2026

Customer pages.

Give your buyers a download page that looks like your brand.

  • NewBranded pages at sovyx.cc/b/yourname: your name, colors, tagline, announcement and links.
  • NewUpload your loader. Buyers need a valid key, and each download link works once for 5 minutes.
  • NewUpload your own logo, or paste a link.
  • NewA Discord bot for purchase and support tickets, key delivery, /redeem and staff commands.
v1.0.0
November 22, 2026

Sovyx is live.

The first public release: the dashboard, the plans and the basics of licensing.

  • NewA dashboard with applications, licenses, users and sessions.
  • NewFree, Basic, Pro and Ultimate plans, bought with a key and redeemed in the dashboard.
  • NewSign in with email and password or with Discord.
  • NewCreate license keys in bulk, ban and unban them, and export them as CSV.
