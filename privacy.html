Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Overview

Sovyx ("we", "us", "our") operates the Sovyx platform at sovyx.cc. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and your rights regarding that data.

By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

3. How we use your data

We use collected data strictly to:

  • Provide and operate the Service — processing orders, delivering digital goods, managing accounts
  • Process payments — billing and subscription management
  • Send service communications — account notifications, delivery confirmations, security alerts
  • Improve the platform — understanding how the Service is used to fix issues and add features
  • Prevent fraud and abuse — detecting suspicious activity and enforcing our Terms
  • Comply with legal obligations — responding to lawful requests from authorities

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We do not use your data for advertising.

4. Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies on our site. Here is what we set:

  • Essential cookies — required for the platform to function (session management, authentication). These cannot be disabled.
  • Preference cookies — store settings you choose (e.g. interface preferences).
  • Analytics cookies — help us understand traffic patterns and usage. We use privacy-respecting analytics that do not track you across other sites.

You can manage non-essential cookies through the cookie banner shown on your first visit, or by adjusting your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some functionality.

5. Third-party services

We work with a small number of trusted third-party providers to operate the Service:

  • Payment processors — handle billing. They operate under their own privacy policies and are PCI-DSS compliant.
  • Cloud hosting providers — host our servers and databases. Data is stored in encrypted form.
  • Email delivery services — send transactional emails on our behalf.

These providers are contractually bound to use your data only for the purposes of providing services to us and not for their own commercial purposes.

6. Data retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide the Service. If you close your account:

  • Account and profile data is deleted within 30 days
  • Transaction records are retained for 7 years to meet legal/tax obligations
  • Anonymised usage data may be retained indefinitely for analytics

7. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectification — request correction of inaccurate data
  • Erasure — request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Objection — object to processing of your data for certain purposes
  • Restriction — request that we limit how we process your data

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@sovyx.cc. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest, hashed password storage, and access controls limiting who within Sovyx can view personal data.

No system is 100% secure. If you believe your account has been compromised, contact us immediately at security@sovyx.cc.

9. Children's privacy

The Service is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such data, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting an updated version on this page and updating the "Last updated" date. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance.

11. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests: privacy@sovyx.cc
