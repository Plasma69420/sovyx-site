Affiliates

Earn when you send sellers to Sovyx.

Know people who sell software? Give them your link. You earn a share of every plan they buy.

Join in Discord

1. Join

Type /affiliate in our Discord and press Join. You get a personal link and code straight away.

2. Share it

Send your link, or tell people to enter your code when they open a purchase ticket. Your link shows them a reminder to do it.

3. Get paid

When staff confirm a buyer's payment, your commission is added to your balance. Check it any time with /affiliate, and ask staff to pay it out.

The details

  • Commission: the standard rate is 20% of the plan price for every sale you refer. Staff can set a different rate for individual partners.
  • What counts: sales of Sovyx plans (Basic, Pro and Ultimate) where the buyer used your code and their payment was confirmed. Each purchase is credited once.
  • Not credited: your own purchases, and sales that are refunded.
  • Payouts: staff pay out on request. Agree the method in a ticket. You can see what you have earned, been paid and are still owed at any time.

Ground rules

  • Be honest about what Sovyx does. Do not make promises we don't make.
  • No spam and no unsolicited mass messages.
  • Do not pretend to be Sovyx staff.

We can pause or remove anyone who breaks these rules, and commissions from misleading sales may be removed.

Ready to start?

Open the Discord, type /affiliate and you will have your link in seconds.
