How it works

Up and running in three steps.

No servers to host. Sovyx keeps the keys, the sessions and the downloads for you.

1

Create an application

Make one in your dashboard. You get an app ID and a branded page where your buyers download your loader.

2

Add one call to your software

Sell and stay in control

Make keys, give people their own panels, reset HWIDs and end sessions, all from one place.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need to host anything?

No. Sovyx stores your keys, runs the sign-in and sessions, and serves your download page. You only add the login call to your software.

How does the HWID lock work?

The first time a key signs in, it locks to that PC. The same key on another PC is refused until the lock is reset. You can turn the lock off per application.

Can buyers reset their own HWID?

Yes, from your customer page or the API. You choose whether it's allowed and how many days they must wait between resets. You and your staff can always reset it from the dashboard.

How do resellers work?

You give a person a panel for a product with a credit balance and an end date. They spend credits to make keys, priced by length. You decide whether they can also reset HWIDs, see sessions or read the activity log.

Can I run more than one product?

Yes. Each application has its own keys, users, sessions and customer page. How many you can create depends on your plan.

How do I pay for a paid plan?

Open a ticket in our Discord, pay, and you receive a key for the plan. Redeem it in your dashboard and the plan switches on. The Free plan needs no card.
