Resellers and staff

Your panel, your access.

If someone gave you a panel for their product, here is how it works.

Log in

1. Get a panel

A seller gives you a panel for one of their products, with an end date and, if you can make keys, a credit balance.

2. Sign in

Use the normal Sovyx login with your email or Discord. Your panels appear under My panels.

3. Get to work

Depending on your access: download the product, make keys with credits, reset HWIDs, ban keys, watch live sessions and read the activity log.

Want to give panels to your own resellers?

Panels let you share a product without sharing your dashboard. Each person gets exactly what you choose: keys from a credit balance, HWID resets, sessions, an activity log, and an end date.

Panels are set up for you by the Sovyx team for now. Ask in the Discord and we will get you going.

Need help with a panel?

Ask in the Discord and mention which product it's for.
