Refund Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Overview

This policy covers refunds for Sovyx platform subscriptions. It does not govern the digital goods transactions your end-customers make through your Sovyx shop — that is governed by your own shop's policies.

Important: Due to the digital nature of the Service and the immediate access granted upon purchase, all Sovyx subscription fees are generally non-refundable. Please review your chosen plan carefully before subscribing.

No refunds — standard cases

Refunds will not be issued for:

  • Change of mind after purchase
  • Failure to cancel before the renewal date
  • Partial use of a billing period — if you cancel mid-month, your access continues until the period ends, with no pro-rated refund
  • Downgrading from a higher plan
  • Account suspension or termination due to violation of our Terms of Service

Exceptions — when we do refund

We will process a refund if any of the following apply and can be verified.
  • Duplicate charge — your account was charged more than once for the same billing period due to a technical error on our end
  • Charge after cancellation — your account was charged after a confirmed cancellation
  • Service outage — the platform experienced a verified outage lasting more than 72 continuous hours that substantially prevented you from using the Service
  • Incorrect plan — you were charged for a plan different from the one you selected, through no fault of your own

Refund requests under these exceptions must be submitted within 14 days of the charge date.

How to request a refund

To request a refund, email billing@sovyx.cc with:

  • Your account email address
  • The charge date and amount
  • A brief description of the issue and why you believe a refund applies
  • Any supporting evidence (e.g. screenshots of duplicate charges)

We aim to respond to all refund requests within 5 business days. Approved refunds are processed back to your original payment method and may take 5–10 business days to appear, depending on your bank.

Free trial

If Sovyx offers a free trial period for a plan, no charge is made during the trial. You may cancel at any time before the trial ends to avoid being charged. A reminder will be sent before the trial period expires.

Contact

Billing questions or refund requests: billing@sovyx.cc
